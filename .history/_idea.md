# Raw Idea Notes

There is a certain amount of players that can play on the same tablet for example which is put in the middle and then it goes in rounds and each round the players have their turn in a clockwise fashion, one after the other. During the setup of the game, the players can select their color (they will be referred by the game by this color henceforth - e.g. "Player Red has won") and their *expertise*. Each player can select a few categories in which they believe they are particularly strong.

The game starts. Each player turn consists of two phases. The first is the question selection where they are presented with four possible questions. Each question is represented by an ominous title, which hints at what the question might be but does not give it away completely. More importantly, the first question in the selection is always sampled from the players expertise categories. Answering this question will be less rewarding - I will get into the reward system later on. The next two questions are random questions from all of the corpus and with random difficulty. They are more rewarding. The last question is always a hard or a very hard questions also randomly from the corpus. This will be the most rewarding (since it is the highest risk) and will also provide a special boon.

The reward system. Next I will describe how to win the game. The game is won on a "bingo board" each player has a 4x4 or 5x5 (needs to be configurable in the game setup) board of pegs that can be populated. The board has columns identified by letters and rows by numbers - so a specific field can be identified like A3. Once pegs in a diagonal, horizontal or vertical line are collected that player wins the game. All questions and special events award pegs on this board. The first question - the expertise question - awards a completely random peg anywhere on the field (therefore would take quite long to assemble the board). The two middle questions award a random peg in a random row and column - so they might say on the selection screen (B,3); meaning the random peg is either anywhere in column B or row 3. The last question lets the player choose the row and column where the random choice should take place. Additionally the last question also awards a special joker.

Jokers. Each player should start with 4 jokers. Namely: 1. Reshuffle the question choice on the question choice screen. 2. Reshuffle a question once it has been selected (from the same category). 3. Reveal a hint about the question. 4. "The Gambler" - you stake to loose a random peg on your board, get a completely random question you cant anticipate, but if you win you get 3 random pegs on the board. But there are also special jokers which can be gained by completing the hard questions. One could be to steal a random peg from an enemy. One could be to curse an enemy to make all the next question forcibly from the "hard" difficulty. Stuff like this. There should also be a mechanic to get jokers in a different way but I will think about this later.

The questions. Questions should come in 4 difficulties: easy, medium, hard and very hard. The bulk of the questions is supposed to be just the usual multiple choice answer questions, as they usually are. But there should also be special questions possible like sorting things by a metric, locating things on a map (most suitable to geography questions, but also for example "where was paper invented" in science questions or something like that) or even something like calculating certain things in the science questions, or listening questions for music "play the first 10 seconds of a song and what is it?"
