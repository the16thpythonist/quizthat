## Key Facts:
- Novak Djokovic holds the record for most ATP Masters 1000 titles with 40 (as of 2025)
- He is the only player to complete the Career Golden Masters (winning all nine Masters 1000 events)
- He completed this feat for the first time in 2018 at the Cincinnati Open
- He completed it a second time in 2020, also at Cincinnati
- The nine Masters 1000 tournaments are: Indian Wells, Miami, Monte-Carlo, Madrid, Rome, Canada, Cincinnati, Shanghai, and Paris
- Rafael Nadal is second with 36 Masters 1000 titles
- Djokovic won 6 Masters 1000 titles in a single season (2015), which is a record

The Cincinnati Masters (officially the Western & Southern Open) is held annually in Mason, Ohio, and was indeed the tournament that completed Djokovic's historic Career Golden Masters achievement in 2018.
