## Galaxy Classification Research

Based on research from NASA, ESA/Hubble, and astronomical sources:

### Edwin Hubble's Classification System
- Developed in 1926 as the "tuning fork" diagram
- Elliptical galaxies classified E0 through E7 based on ellipticity
- E0 = nearly round, E7 = highly elongated/flattened
- The number represents the ratio of major to minor axis
- Spiral galaxies classified Sa, Sb, Sc (tightness of spiral arms)
- S0 = lenticular (transition between elliptical and spiral)

### Key Facts Used
- Hubble classification is fundamental to galaxy astronomy
- Ellipticity is the defining characteristic of this numbering system
- Medium difficulty: requires knowledge beyond basic galaxy types
- Distractors are plausible but definitively incorrect
