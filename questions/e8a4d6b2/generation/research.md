# Research Notes: Sound Speed in Wood

## Key Physics Findings

Based on research from multiple scientific sources, wood exhibits highly anisotropic acoustic properties:

### Speed Measurements
- **Parallel to grain**: 5000-6000 m/s
- **Perpendicular to grain**: 1000-2000 m/s  
- **Ratio**: Approximately 5-6 times faster parallel vs perpendicular

### Physical Explanation
Wood's acoustic anisotropy results from its cellular structure and fiber orientation. The aligned cellulose fibers provide a more direct pathway for sound wave propagation when traveling parallel to the grain direction.

### Factors Affecting Acoustic Properties
- Grain orientation (primary factor)
- Moisture content
- Temperature  
- Wood density
- Species-specific cellular structure
