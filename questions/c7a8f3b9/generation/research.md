# Research Notes: Sand Physics - Angle of Repose

## Key Findings
- **Angle of repose**: The steepest angle at which granular material can be piled without slumping
- **Typical range for dry sand**: 30-35 degrees  
- **Factors affecting angle**: Grain size, shape, surface roughness, and moisture content
- **Grain shape impact**: Smooth, rounded grains have lower angles than rough, angular grains
- **Physics principle**: Balance between gravitational forces and inter-grain friction
