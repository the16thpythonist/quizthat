# Ocean Sound Speed Research

## Key Facts
- Sound speed in seawater ranges from 1,450-1,570 m/s (typically ~1500 m/s)
- Sound speed in air is approximately 330 m/s  
- This makes sound travel about 5 times faster in water than in air
- Speed depends on temperature, salinity, and pressure (depth)

## Factors Affecting Sound Speed in Ocean
- **Temperature**: +4.5 m/s per 1°C increase
- **Salinity**: +1.3 m/s per 1 psu increase  
- **Pressure**: +1.7 m/s per 100m depth increase
